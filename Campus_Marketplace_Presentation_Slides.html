PROJECT CONTEXT & MOTIVATION

The Problem in Sri Lankan Universities

⚠️ Current Status Quo & Chaos
  • Scattered Channels: Students rely on unorganized WhatsApp/Telegram groups and Facebook batches to buy/sell essentials.
  • Lost in the Feed: Important academic equipment listings (calculators, drafters, lab coats, textbooks) vanish under 500+ daily chat messages.
  • Trust & Safety Risks: No reputation history, zero verified reviews, and risky off-campus meeting arrangements with unknown people.
  • Severe Academic Waste: Graduating seniors throw away costly course material while freshers struggle to find affordable equipment.
🎯 Our Solution: Campus Marketplace
  • Hyper-Localized Discovery: Purpose-built for Sri Lankan universities with filtered faculties and trusted campus communities.
  • Student-Friendly Price Discovery: Built-in structured offer negotiation so students can agree on fair second-hand prices.
  • Campus Safe Zones: Designated physical handover spots (University Library, Main Canteen, Faculty Complex) for zero-risk handoffs.
  • Scam Shield & Accountability: Peer-reviewed seller reputation and one-tap scam reporting keeps the marketplace honest.
SYSTEM DESIGN & TECHNOLOGY

System Architecture & Tech Stack

📱 Frontend Client

React Native with Expo SDK 57

  • Cross-platform for Android & iOS devices.
  • Instant hot reloading with Expo Go testing.
  • Componentized UI design with Lucide icons.
  • Custom responsive navigation bar.
🔥 Cloud Backend

Firebase & Cloud Storage

  • Firestore NoSQL: Real-time collections for listings, messages, offers, and requests.
  • Cloud Storage: Fast image hosting for item photos with CDN caching.
  • Auth Engine: Secure university student credentials and session tokens.
🛡️ Engineering Standards

TypeScript Strict Architecture

  • Zero any leakage across all core entities.
  • Synchronous view switching to prevent native modal deadlocks.
  • Strict prop interfaces in types.ts ensuring contract integrity.
TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHT & CHALLENGES SOLVED

From Base Template to Production-Ready App

Area Initial Template State Final Engineering Upgrade
Android Modal Freeze (Critical) Defect Clicking review/offer opened nested <Modal> inside another modal, locking Android touch dispatch completely. Engineered Solution Replaced stacked native dialogs with single-parent view routing (activeView state) + instant ← Back breadcrumbs.
Bargaining & Offers Missing Only passive chat messaging without price settlement. Engineered Solution MakeOfferModal with quick percentage pills (-10%, -15%, -20%) and live offer statuses.
Physical Meetup Safety Missing No designated coordination for in-person item exchanges. Engineered Solution MeetupSchedulerModal with campus safe-zone presets and safety advisories.
Community Trust & Quality Vulnerable No reputation check, open to unmoderated fraudulent posts. Engineered Solution 5-star seller ratings + ReportListingModal scam shield with instant categorization.

EVALUATION & ROADMAP

Viva Q&A Defense & Future Enhancements

🎓 Anticipated Examiner Viva Questions
  • Why choose Expo & React Native over native Android?
    Ensures rapid cross-platform deployment on both Android and iOS from a single TypeScript codebase.
  • How did you resolve the Android touch freeze?
    Eliminated nested native modal stacking that caused Android Dialog window deadlocks; replaced with internal view state routing.
  • How does this scale across multiple universities?
    Multi-tenant Firestore schema partitioned by University ID (e.g., UoM, UoC, UoP).
🚀 Future Project Roadmap
  • Institutional SSO: Integration with official university email domains (.ac.lk) for 100% verified student identity.
  • LankaQR Escrow Payments: In-app digital escrow holding funds securely until both parties verify physical item receipt.
  • Push Notifications: Real-time device alerts for incoming buyer offers, chat messages, and price drop notifications.
